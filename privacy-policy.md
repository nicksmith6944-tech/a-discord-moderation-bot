# ArcLight Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 10, 2026

ArcLight ("ArcLight", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") is a Discord moderation bot designed to provide moderation, logging, and server-management features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ArcLight processes, why it is processed, how it is stored, and how users or server administrators can request its deletion.

## 1. Information We Process

When ArcLight is installed on or used in a Discord server, it may process the following information:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server (guild) IDs
* Discord member information required for moderation features
* Warning counts associated with users
* Moderation information, including:

  * Moderator ID
  * Target user ID
  * Moderation action
  * Date and time of the action
* Forced nickname information, including the relevant user ID, server ID, and nickname
* Information provided as part of moderation commands, such as moderation reasons

ArcLight may also receive message content and message-related information when required to perform moderation commands. ArcLight does not intentionally store the contents of ordinary Discord messages in its database.

## 2. Why We Process This Information

ArcLight processes this information only to provide and operate its Discord features, including:

* Warnings and moderation
* Bans and kicks
* Mutes and jail-related moderation
* Moderation logging
* Server moderation statistics
* Forced nickname functionality
* Message cleanup and related moderation functions
* Maintaining moderation records for server staff

## 3. Moderation Logs

When a moderation action is performed, ArcLight may record information such as the moderator, target user, action type, reason, and timestamp.

Depending on the server's configuration, moderation information may also be posted in the server's designated moderation-log channel.

Moderation records are intended to help server staff review and manage moderation activity.

## 4. Data Storage

ArcLight uses a database to store certain moderation-related information.

The database may contain Discord IDs, warning counts, moderation records, timestamps, and forced nickname information.

ArcLight does not sell personal information, use stored information for advertising, or intentionally share stored information with unrelated third parties.

## 5. Data Retention

Moderation information may remain stored for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide moderation and server-management functionality.

Server administrators may request deletion of data associated with their server. Individual users may also request deletion of information associated with their Discord account where applicable.

Deletion requests can be made using the contact information below.

ArcLight operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform and privacy policies.

ArcLight does not intentionally sell or transfer stored user information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Discord's own handling of information is governed by Discord's policies and is separate from ArcLight's handling of information.

## 8. Children's Privacy

ArcLight is a Discord bot and does not intentionally collect additional personal information from users beyond information made available through Discord and information required for its features.

ArcLight does not knowingly use collected information for advertising or profiling.

## 9. Security
