ArcLight Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

ArcLight is a Discord moderation and server-management bot. This Privacy Policy explains what information ArcLight processes and why.

1. Information We Process

ArcLight may process the following information when used in a Discord server:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server (Guild) IDs
  • Warning counts
  • Moderator IDs
  • Target user IDs
  • Moderation action types
  • Moderation timestamps
  • Forced nickname information
  • Information provided as part of moderation commands, such as reasons

2. Why We Process This Information

Information is processed to provide ArcLight's moderation and server-management features, including:

  • Warnings
  • Bans and kicks
  • Mute and jail functionality
  • Moderation logging
  • Moderation statistics
  • Forced nicknames
  • Message cleanup and moderation functions

3. Message Content

ArcLight may access message content when required for certain moderation commands. Message content is not intentionally stored in ArcLight's database.

4. Moderation Logs

When a moderation action is performed, ArcLight may record information such as the moderator, target user, action, reason, and timestamp.

Moderation information may also be sent to the server's designated moderation-log channel.

5. Data Storage

ArcLight stores certain moderation-related information in a database, including Discord IDs, warning counts, moderation records, timestamps, and forced nickname information.

ArcLight does not sell stored information or use it for advertising.

6. Data Retention

Moderation information may be retained for as long as it is reasonably required to provide moderation and server-management functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

ArcLight operates through Discord. Discord independently processes information according to its own policies.

For more information, see Discord's Privacy Policy.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by ArcLight. However, no electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ArcLight's functionality or data practices change. The last updated date will be changed when significant changes are made.
